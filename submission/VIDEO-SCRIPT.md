# 视频脚本

全程只录浏览器一个窗口。约 224 词 ≈ **1 分 32 秒**，排程 2:05，留 30 秒余量。

---

## 0:00 – 0:15 · 开场

**动作：** 点 `▶ Start`，开始念

> Older people send the money themselves, so the bank sees nothing wrong. In 2024 older Americans reported losing almost five billion dollars. By the time anyone notices, the money is gone.

---

## 0:15 – 0:32 · 三档决策

**动作：** 鼠标依次指 绿 → 橙 → 红 三行，各停 1 秒。只移动，不点击。

> We score every transfer before it goes through, and return one of three answers.
>
> Approve — let it go.
>
> Confirm — pause it, explain it in plain words, and alert the family.
>
> Block — stop it, and keep a record.

---

## 0:32 – 1:00 · 点开一个案例

**动作：** 点一下那行红色的 BLOCK

> This is running live on IBM LinuxONE.
>
> Let me open a blocked one. *(点击)*
>
> These are the transfers behind that decision. Same account, minutes apart, and the amount keeps climbing. That is what a scam looks like in the data.

> 不念具体数字。每次点开的笔数和金额都不同，念错就露馅。

---

## 1:00 – 1:32 · 模型、解释、IBM Z

**动作：** 先指顶部 `IBM LinuxONE · s390x`，再指右侧解释卡

> Two models read it. One reads the last seven transfers as a sequence. The other reads the numbers. We combine them, and the answer comes back in about twenty milliseconds.
>
> IBM Granite then writes a message the customer can understand, and an alert for the family. It all runs on this machine, so no data leaves the bank.
>
> This is s390x, with an AI accelerator built into the processor. That is why it stays this fast.

---

## 1:32 – 1:45 · 结果

**动作：** 无

> On a full replay of 587 transfers, we catch about two thirds of the fraud, and almost everything we stop is real.

> 说约数，不报精确值 —— 顶部计数器是这次的实时累计，和完整一轮对不上。

---

## 1:45 – 2:05 · 收尾

**动作：** 无。说完停 2 秒再停录。

> For the customer, a few more seconds to decide. For the family, an alert they can act on.
>
> Thank you.
